Frequently Asked

Questions & Answers

Do I need any prior cooking experience to enroll?+
Not at all. Our short courses and diploma programs are designed for all levels — from absolute beginners to experienced cooks who want to sharpen their professional skills.
What's the difference between the Short Courses and the Diploma?+
Short courses (starting July 13) are focused 1–2 week masterclasses on specific skills. Diploma programs (starting August 10) are comprehensive 1–6 month tracks covering full cuisine, patisserie, and business.
Are there installment payment options?+
Yes. Contact our admissions team on WhatsApp (+234 810 140 8693) to discuss flexible payment arrangements before the cohort starts.
What certificate do I receive on completion?+
You receive a Lyndishes Culinary School certificate recognised by employers and hospitality institutions. Diploma graduates also receive an industry endorsement letter.
Where is the school located?+
We are located in Gwarimpa, Abuja, Nigeria. Exact directions are shared with enrolled students via WhatsApp.
Can I enroll from outside Abuja?+
Yes — many of our students relocate for training. We can help connect you with recommended accommodation options near the school.
What happens after I pay?+
You'll receive confirmation instantly. Our admissions team will reach out within 24 hours via WhatsApp to share your timetable, kit list, and orientation details.
Is the Entrepreneurship class only for chefs?+
No. Anyone who wants to start or grow a food business — caterers, home bakers, restaurateurs — will benefit. It covers pricing, menu design, marketing, and running a profitable kitchen.
