About Lyndishes

The Fastest Way to Start a Profitable Food Business in Abuja

Founded by Chef Lynda Chinasa Ewalefo, Lyndishes Culinary School (LCS) was built on a simple premise: cooking passion isn't enough. You need the skills to cook perfectly, and the business acumen to make it profitable.

As a City & Guilds certified instructor trained at ICCA Dubai, Chef Lynda brings world-class standards to our Gwarimpa campus. We don't just teach recipes — we teach food costing, menu engineering, and brand building.
